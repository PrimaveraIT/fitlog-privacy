Help and support

Can’t find your answer below? Email info@primavera-it.be. We usually reply within 1 to 2 working days.

Contact

  • Email: info@primavera-it.be (questions, problems, ideas).
  • Abuse or inappropriate content: report it in the app via ⋯ or by pressing and holding. We review reports within 24 hours.
  • When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model and FitLog version (Settings › About › Version).

Account and sign-in

  • You create an account with Apple or Google. It is optional: your workouts and nutrition work without one too.
  • Signing in with Apple creates a new account if you choose “Hide My Email”. Want to use your existing account? Choose “Share My Email”, or sign in with Google using the same email address.
  • Deleting your account: Profile › Delete account. Your profile, posts, photos and cloud backup are erased right away.

Your data

  • Your workouts, food and photos are on your phone. If you delete the app without a backup, they are gone.
  • Automatic backup: Settings › Backup. On a new phone, sign in with the same account; the app then offers to restore your backup.
  • Keep or move everything in one file: Settings › Your data › Export (and Import to restore).

Training

  • Tap ✓ on an empty set to take today’s target: per exercise, FitLog suggests whether to go heavier, add a rep, repeat or take a deload.
  • Tap the set number for a warm-up set (W); press and hold it to delete the set.
  • Workouts are saved automatically, even if you forget to finish.
  • A new record is celebrated right away: heavier than ever, or more reps than your best estimated 1RM.

Nutrition and goals

  • Scan a barcode or search for a product. Not found? Enter it yourself; next time FitLog recognises it.
  • Change your calorie and macro goals by tapping your macro overview in Nutrition. You can also have them recalculated there from your weight and goal.
  • The calculations are estimates, not medical advice.

Friends, challenges and coaching

  • Find friends: in Feed, tap 👤+ at the top right and search by @username.
  • Block or report someone: open their profile and tap ⋯.
  • Challenges and the weekly leaderboard: Feed › 🏆. Joining the leaderboard is optional.
  • Coaching: your coach gives you a 6-character code; enter it under Profile › Have a coach?. You can stop under Profile › Stop with this coach, and everything you shared is erased.

Troubleshooting

  • Something doesn’t look up to date? Pull the screen down to refresh, or fully close the app and open it again.
  • No notifications? Turn them on in your iPhone’s Settings › Notifications › FitLog.
  • Camera or photos not working? Allow access in your iPhone’s Settings › Apps › FitLog.
  • Change the app’s language in FitLog under Settings › Language.
