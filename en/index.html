FitLog

Privacy policy

Updated on 27 November 2026

This is a translation. In case of differences, the Dutch version prevails.

In short

  • Your workouts, food, weight and progress photos stay on your own phone.
  • With an account we only store what is needed to sign in, follow friends and share workouts.
  • Cloud backup is off by default. If you turn it on, we ask for your consent first.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Only anonymous crash reports, to fix bugs. We sell nothing.
  • You can always export your data and delete your account, right in the app.

Who is responsible?

FitLog is made by Christophe Veracchia, the data controller within the meaning of the GDPR. Questions about privacy? Email info@primavera-it.be.

What stays on your phone only

This never reaches our server, unless you turn on the backup yourself (see below):

  • workouts, sets, weights, notes and plans
  • food, meals and your nutrition goals
  • body weight, and your age, height and sex (only to calculate your calories)
  • progress photos (not in your camera roll either: they live in the app’s own folder)
  • reminders and settings

What we store when you create an account

  • Account: your email address and your password (stored only in encrypted form, we cannot read it).
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, we only get your name and email address from them (with Apple you can choose a hidden relay address). No password, contacts or other data. Your name only pre-fills the profile form; you choose what to show.
  • Profile: name, username, bio, profile photo and whether your profile is private or public.
  • Friends: who you follow, who follows you, requests and who you block.
  • What you share: posts with a photo, text and (if you choose) a summary of your workout, plus 💪 and comments.

A private profile and its posts are only visible to people you approve. With a public profile, any FitLog user can see your posts.

Cloud backup (optional)

If you turn on automatic backup in Settings, we store a compressed copy of your workouts, plans, food, weight, body data (age, height, sex) and settings in your account. Progress photos are not included.

Weight and workout data can be health data. That is why we only do this with your explicit consent, which you give by turning on the backup. You can withdraw it at any time: turn off the backup and delete it with one tap. Only you have access to your backup.

Leaderboard and challenges (optional)

If you join the leaderboard, we share your weekly numbers (number of workouts, sets, volume in kg and your streak in weeks) with the people allowed to see your posts. No weight, no food, no per-exercise details. If you turn it off, we delete your weekly numbers from the server.

In a challenge, participants see each other’s progress for that challenge (e.g. number of workouts in December). You can only invite people you follow, and you can always decline an invite or leave.

Coaching (optional)

If you enter a coach’s code, you first see who it is and what you share. Only after your explicit consent does the app share with that one coach: your age, height, sex, goal and activity level, your weight over the last 4 months, your daily goals and a summary of your workouts over the last 2 months.

If you do the weekly check-in, only your coach also receives your weight for that week, three scores (week, nutrition, energy), your note and optionally a photo. You never share your food diary, progress photos or workout notes. Your coach can send you a plan or daily goals; you choose whether to apply them. If you stop with your coach (or the coach stops), we immediately erase everything that was shared, including your check-ins and check-in photos.

If you are a coach yourself, you are responsible for how you use your clients’ data: only to guide them, and not to store it outside FitLog or pass it on.

Reports and moderation

If you report a post, comment or profile, we store:

  • who reported, what was reported, the reason and any explanation you gave;
  • what a moderator did with it (nothing wrong, hidden or deleted).

The person you report does not see who reported. Only FitLog moderators see the report, to handle it within 24 hours. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe (and the App Store rules). Reports are erased as soon as the account of the reporter or of the reported person is deleted.

Whatever you report is hidden for you right away. That list is stored only on your phone.

Crash reports

If something goes wrong in the app (a crash or an error), the app automatically sends a technical report to Sentry so we can fix it. Such a report contains:

  • what went wrong and where in the code;
  • your device model, the iOS and FitLog version, and the language;
  • the last screens you opened, without names, email addresses or IDs.

Nothing from your workouts, food, weight, photos, messages, name or email address is ever included, and no IP address. The app removes such data from every report before it is sent. Reports are not linked to your account.

Why we may do this (legal basis)

  • Account, profile, friends and sharing: necessary to provide the service you ask for (contract).
  • Backup with health data: your explicit consent.
  • Sharing with your coach: your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Leaderboard and challenges: your choice to take part (consent), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Security and preventing abuse, for example blocking someone: our legitimate interest.
  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and safe, with as little data as possible.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and storage) stores your account data for us, as a processor under a data processing agreement. The database and storage are in the EU: Western Europe (Ireland, eu-west-1). For the exceptional cases where Supabase has access from outside the EU, for example for support, the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses apply.
  • Open Food Facts (an open, free food database): when you scan a barcode or search for a product, we only send that barcode or search term, without your name or account. As with any website, their server does see your IP address.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) receives the anonymous crash reports, as a processor under a data processing agreement. The reports are stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany); for any access from outside the EU, the standard contractual clauses apply.
  • Apple, if you install the app through the App Store, under Apple’s privacy policy.

We do not sell data and share nothing with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep data

  • As long as your account exists. A post or comment you delete is gone immediately.
  • Your cloud backup until you delete it or delete your account.
  • What you share with your coach: until you stop with that coach. It is refreshed each time you open the app; we do not keep older versions.
  • If you delete your account, we immediately erase your profile, posts, photos, comments, follow relationships, backup and login. Technical server backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Crash reports: at most 90 days, after which Sentry deletes them automatically.
  • What is on your phone disappears when you delete the app.

Your rights

You can do most of it yourself, right in the app:

  • Access and portability: Settings › Your data › Export (one file with all your data).
  • Correction: Profile › Edit.
  • Deletion: Profile › Delete account.
  • Withdrawing consent: turn off the backup and delete it.
  • For anything else, such as objection or restriction, email info@primavera-it.be. We reply within one month.
  • Not satisfied? You can always lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be) or the supervisory authority in your own country.

Security

All traffic with the server is encrypted (HTTPS) and storage is encrypted. On the server, each user can only change their own data; this is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), not just by the app. Photos in posts are in private storage and are only shown to those allowed to see them.

Age

You must be at least 16 years old to create an account.

Changes

If something important changes, for example because the app starts using new data, we update this policy and let you know in the app before it applies.
